## Acceptance criteria (if the surface is verified)

- One **real** x402 payment settles on Base USDC — sub-dollar, genuine, not simulated.
- x402 uses EIP-3009 `TransferWithAuthorization`; the facilitator pays gas, so no ETH is needed for
  the payment itself.
- Payment authorisations use **random 32-byte nonces** and are order-independent. These are
  categorically distinct from the account's sequential execution nonce, which KeeperHub manages.
  **The ledger keeps them separate and Convoy sets neither.**
- Server-side Turnkey caps are the real guardrail and are respected: USDC allowlist,
  ≤100 USDC per transfer, 200 USDC per UTC-day (a 429 `DAILY_CAP_EXCEEDED` is expected behaviour,
  not an error to route around).
- The HMAC secret lives at `~/.keeperhub/wallet.json` with mode `0600`, on the worker only. It is
  never committed and never shipped to the browser.
- The budget meter shows a real $0.01–$0.05 debit traceable to a settlement.
